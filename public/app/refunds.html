Refund Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version

  • AI Doctor Study is 100% free. There is no Pro tier and nothing to buy, so there is nothing to refund.
  • We don't take payments, store card details, or bill anyone for using the app.
  • A fair-use rate limit keeps the AI features running smoothly for everyone — it is not a paid quota.

01No billing, no refunds

AI Doctor Study used to offer a paid Pro subscription through Gumroad. That subscription no longer exists — every feature is free for every user, with no plans to reintroduce payments. Since nothing is purchased, this policy is simple: there is nothing to request a refund for.

02If you were previously charged

If you paid for a Pro subscription before it was discontinued and still see a charge or active subscription, cancel it directly from your Gumroad subscriptions page or the link in your Gumroad receipt email — that stops any future billing immediately. For a refund on a past charge, contact Gumroad support or email support@aidoctor.study and we'll help sort it out.

03Changes to this policy

We may update this page if anything about pricing ever changes. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version.

04Contact

Anything unclear? Email support@aidoctor.study.
